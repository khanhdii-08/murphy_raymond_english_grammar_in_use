1.1 · Mục tiêu & bản chất

Unit này gom 5 cách dùng tính từ + to V: (A) difficult to understand, (B) nice of you to…, (C) sorry / glad to… (phản ứng cảm xúc), (D) the first / last / only … to…, (E) bound / sure / likely to…. Bẫy lớn nhất nằm ở phần A: không lặp lại tân ngữ ở cuối câu.

It's difficult to understand him. = He is difficult to understand.
Khi "he" đã lên làm chủ ngữ thì KHÔNG nói He is difficult to understand him.

1.2 · Công thức cốt lõi

(A) It's + adj + to V + O = O + is + adj + to V (không lặp O) It's easy to use this app. = This app is easy to use. a + adj + N + to V a difficult question to answer (B) It's + adj + of + sb + to V It was kind of you to help. (C) S + be + adj (cảm xúc) + to V I'm sorry to hear that. (D) the first / last / next / only (+ N) + to V the first person to arrive (E) S + be + sure / certain / bound / likely + to V It's likely to rain.
MụcTính từ hay gặpVí dụ
Aeasy, hard, difficult, nice, safe, cheap, expensive, interesting, impossible, dangerousThis water is safe to drink.
Bnice, kind, generous, mean, careless, silly, stupid, unfair, (in)considerateIt's silly of him to quit.
Csorry, glad, pleased, relieved, surprised, amazed, sad, disappointedWe were glad to get home.
Dthe first / second / last / next / onlyShe was the only one to pass.
Esure, certain, bound, likelyHe's bound to win.

1.3 · A. Difficult to understand…

  • It's hard to please my boss. → My boss is hard to please. (không nói hard to please him)
  • Is it safe (for us) to swim here? → Is this beach safe (for us) to swim at?
  • The exam questions were impossible to answer. (không nói to answer them)
  • Với tính từ + danh từ: It was a difficult decision (for me) to make.
  • Giữ giới từ cuối câu: Mai is interesting to talk to.

1.4 · B. Nice of you to…

  • It's + adj + OF + người + to V — nhận xét về tính cách/hành vi của người đó: It was kind of you to lend me your car.
  • So sánh với for: It's easy for me to do it (việc đó dễ với tôi) ↔ It's kind of you to do it (bạn tử tế).

1.5 · C. Sorry to… / surprised to…

  • Diễn tả phản ứng trước một sự việc: I'm sorry to hear that. · Were you surprised to see her? · I was relieved to find my wallet.
  • Câu chào quen thuộc: (I'm) pleased / nice to meet you.

1.6 · D. The first / the last / the only … to…

  • You'll be the first (person) to know. · The next bus to arrive will be the number 32. · I was the only one to arrive on time.
  • to V ở đây thay cho mệnh đề quan hệ: the first person who walked on the moon = the first person to walk on the moon.

1.7 · E. Sure / bound / likely to…

be bound to / be sure to / be certain to = chắc chắn sẽ be likely to = có khả năng sẽ be not likely / unlikely to = khó có khả năng She's bound to pass. · It's likely to rain. · He isn't likely to come.

1.8 · Lỗi hay gặp

❌ Sai✅ Đúng
This book is easy to read it.This book is easy to read.
It's kind for you to help me.It's kind of you to help me.
He is the first who to arrive.He is the first to arrive.
It's likely raining later.It's likely to rain later.
I'm sorry for hear that.I'm sorry to hear that.

Liên kết trong sách: Unit 66 (afraid / interested / sorry) · Unit 84C (It …) · Unit 103 (enough / too + adjective).

2.1 · 8 ví dụ thực tế (đời sống · công việc · thi cử)

📱 This app is really easy to use. (đánh giá sản phẩm)
💧 Is the tap water safe to drink here? (du lịch — hỏi thông tin)
🙏 It was very kind of you to pick me up from the airport. (giao tiếp — cảm ơn)
😢 I'm sorry to hear about your grandfather. (chia buồn)
🤝 Pleased to meet you. I've heard a lot about you. (công việc — gặp đối tác)
🏃 Nam was the first person to finish the race. (kể chuyện — the first to)
🌧️ Take a raincoat — it's likely to rain this afternoon. (dự báo)
📈 Prices are bound to rise before Tet. (IELTS — dự đoán xu hướng)

2.2 · Mẹo ghi nhớ 🧠

  • Đã đưa lên đầu thì không lặp ở cuối: This dish is easy to cook (không cần "it").
  • OF = nhận xét con người (kind, silly, careless) · FOR = việc đó với ai (easy, hard, safe).
  • Cảm xúc + to V: sorry / glad / surprised / relieved to + hear / see / find / get…
  • bound to ≈ sure to (chắc chắn) · likely to (có khả năng).
  • the first / last / only + to V = rút gọn của "who + V".

2.3 · Mẹo làm bài thi 🎯

  • Viết lại câu It's adj to V + O → O + is + adj + to V; kiểm tra xóa tân ngữ ở cuối.
  • Câu "Nobody did X before him" → He was the first (person) to X.
  • "It probably won't rain" → It isn't likely to rain. "She will certainly pass" → She's bound / sure to pass.
  • Thấy kind / nice / silly / careless / mean + chỗ trống + you → điền of.
  • Khi chuyển, chú ý chia be theo chủ ngữ mới: Some words are impossible to translate.

2.5 · Áp dụng vào nói & viết hàng ngày ✍️

  • Review sản phẩm: It's easy to set up, cheap to run and nice to look at.
  • Cảm ơn tự nhiên: It was so kind of you to… thay vì chỉ "Thank you".
  • Chia buồn / chúc mừng: I'm sorry to hear… / I'm glad to hear…
  • IELTS Writing Task 1/2: dự đoán với is likely to / is bound to.
