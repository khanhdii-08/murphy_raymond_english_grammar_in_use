1.1 · Mục tiêu & bản chất

Cả ba từ đều dịch là "trong" / "trong khi", nhưng trả lời các câu hỏi khác nhau:

  • for + khoảng thời gian → trả lời "Bao lâu?" (for two hours).
  • during + danh từ (sự kiện/giai đoạn) → trả lời "Khi nào?" (during the film).
  • while + S + V → cũng là "khi nào", nhưng theo sau là mệnh đề (while I was watching).
I fell asleep during the film. I was asleep for half an hour.
I fell asleep while I was watching the film.

1.2 · Công thức cốt lõi

1.3 · A. For và during

  • for + khoảng thời gian (độ dài): We talked for two hours. · I've been waiting for ages. · Are you going away for the weekend?
  • during + danh từ (thời điểm xảy ra): Someone's phone rang during the concert. · We met a lot of people during our trip.
  • Với từ chỉ thời gian (the morning, the night, the summer) dùng được in hoặc during: It must have rained in/during the night.

⚠️ during KHÔNG trả lời "bao lâu": It rained for three days — ❌ during three days.

1.4 · B. During và while

during + danh từwhile + S + V
I fell asleep during the lecture.I fell asleep while I was listening to the lecture.
We met many people during our holiday.We met many people while we were on holiday.
He felt ill during the exam.He felt ill while he was doing the exam.
While you were out, a parcel arrived for you.
Lan cooked dinner while I cleaned the house.

1.5 · C. While + hiện tại cho tương lai

I'm going to Seoul next month. I hope to visit a friend while I'm there. ❌ while I'll be What will you do while you're waiting? ❌ while you'll be waiting

2.1 · 8 ví dụ thực tế (đời sống · công việc · thi cử)

⏳ I've been waiting for 40 minutes! (đời sống — than phiền)
📵 Please turn off your phones during the exam. (thi cử — quy định)
📞 While you were out, your boss called. (công việc — nhắn lại)
✈️ I watched three films during the flight. (du lịch — kể chuyện)
🏖️ We're going to Phu Quoc for a week. (du lịch — kế hoạch)
🍳 I listen to podcasts while I'm cooking. (IELTS Speaking — thói quen)
🌙 It must have rained during the night — the streets are wet. (đời sống — suy luận)
🇰🇷 I hope to visit some friends while I'm in Korea. (tương lai — while + hiện tại)

2.2 · Mẹo ghi nhớ 🧠

  • for = số + đơn vị thời gian (two hours, a week, ages) → "bao lâu".
  • during = "trong suốt/trong lúc" + TÊN sự kiện (the film, the war, my holiday).
  • while = during + câu: muốn nói cả câu S + V → đổi sang while.
  • Mẹo đổi: during my stay in Hue ⇄ while I was staying in Hue.
  • Giống if/when: while + hiện tại khi nói tương lai.

2.3 · Mẹo làm bài thi 🎯

  • Sau chỗ trống có con số + thời gian (three days, half an hour) → for.
  • Sau chỗ trống là the + danh từ (the meeting, the interval, the strike) → during.
  • Sau chỗ trống là chủ ngữ + động từ → while.
  • Bẫy: "___ the week" (ngày thường, so với cuối tuần) → during, không phải for.
  • "___ that time" → during (that time là danh từ).

2.5 · Áp dụng vào nói & viết hàng ngày ✍️

  • Nhắn tin/email: "While you were in the meeting, the client called."
  • IELTS Part 2: "During my trip to Da Lat, I met …" / "I stayed there for a week."
  • Mô tả thói quen đa nhiệm: "I usually listen to music while I'm working."
  • Kế hoạch: "While I'm in Singapore, I'll visit Gardens by the Bay."
  • Quy tắc vàng: bao lâu → for; + danh từ → during; + câu → while.
