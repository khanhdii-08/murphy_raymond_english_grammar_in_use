1.1 · Mục tiêu & bản chất

Unit này dạy cách nói "trông/nghe/cảm thấy như thể là …" — đưa ra ấn tượng, phỏng đoán dựa trên những gì ta thấy, nghe, cảm nhận. Ba cách nói gần như cùng nghĩa: like (thân mật), as if, as though (trang trọng hơn), tất cả + S + V.

You look like you haven't slept. = You look as if you haven't slept. = You look as though you haven't slept.
Nhìn mặt bạn, tôi đoán bạn chưa ngủ.

1.2 · Công thức cốt lõi

look / sound / feel + like / as if / as though + S + V She sounds as if she has a cold. It looks / sounds + like / as if / as though + S + V It looks like it's going to rain. V (hành động) + like / as if / as though + S + V He ran as if his life depended on it. as if + QUÁ KHỨ (was/were, knew…) → điều KHÔNG có thật ở hiện tại He talks as if he knew everything. (nhưng anh ta không biết)
Cấu trúcTheo sauVí dụ
look + tính từadjYou look tired.
look like + danh từN (Unit 117)You look like your dad.
look like / as if + S + Vmệnh đềYou look as if you've seen a ghost.

1.3 · A. Look / sound / feel like …

  • That bridge looks like it's going to collapse.
  • Hung sounded as if he was upset on the phone.
  • I've had a week off, but I don't feel as though I've had a break.

1.4 · B. It looks like … / It sounds like …

Chủ ngữ giả It + look/sound để rút ra kết luận từ tình huống:

Lan is an hour late. It looks like she isn't coming.
Listen to that music! It sounds as if they're having a party next door.
We took coats because it looked as though it was going to snow.

1.5 · C. Mô tả cách ai đó làm việc gì

  • She ran like she was being chased.
  • After the mistake, he carried on as if nothing had happened.
  • They stared at me as though I was crazy.

1.6 · D. As if + quá khứ = điều không thật

Khi nói về hiện tại nhưng điều đó không đúng sự thật, sau as if / as though ta lùi về quá khứ (giống if và wish — Unit 39). Có thể dùng were thay was. Thường không dùng like theo cách này.

He talks as if he knew everything. (thực tế: không biết hết) She treats me as if I were her own daughter. (thực tế: không phải con gái) Why do you speak to me as if I was a child? (tôi không phải trẻ con) I remember it as if it was yesterday.

1.7 · So sánh với cấu trúc dễ nhầm

❌ Sai✅ Đúng
You look like tired.You look tired.
It looks as if rain.It looks as if it's going to rain.
He talks as if he knows everything. (ý mỉa: không thật)He talks as if he knew everything.
She looks as she has been crying.She looks as if she has been crying.
I feel like I run a marathon. (vừa chạy 1 km)I feel like I've run a marathon.

Liên kết trong sách: Unit 39C (if I was/were) · Unit 99C (look/sound + tính từ) · Unit 117 (like và as).

2.1 · 8 ví dụ thực tế (đời sống · công việc · thi cử)

🌧️ It looks like it's going to rain. Take an umbrella. (đời sống — thời tiết)
😴 You look as if you haven't slept for days. (giao tiếp — hỏi han)
📞 You sound like you're having a great time! (gọi điện — bạn đi du lịch)
🏃 I feel as though I've run a marathon. (than mệt)
🚑 It looks like there's been an accident. (đường phố — phỏng đoán)
🙄 He talks as if he were the boss. (công việc — điều không thật)
💼 It sounds like the project is going well. (họp — nhận xét báo cáo)
🕰️ I remember our first date as if it was yesterday. (IELTS Part 2 — kỷ niệm)

2.2 · Mẹo ghi nhớ 🧠

  • look + adj · look like + N · look like / as if + S + V — ba "tầng" cần nhớ.
  • like = as if = as though (+ S + V); like thân mật nhất, as though trang trọng nhất.
  • "Như thể" mà KHÔNG thật → as if + quá khứ (were/knew/had).
  • It looks like … = "Có vẻ là …" — câu mở đầu phỏng đoán cực kỳ thông dụng.
  • Việc đã xảy ra trước đó → dùng hiện tại hoàn thành: You look like you've seen a ghost.

2.3 · Mẹo làm bài thi 🎯

  • Sau look/sound/feel: có S + V → like/as if; chỉ có tính từ → không thêm like.
  • Câu mỉa mai/không thật (talks as if he ___ everything) → chọn quá khứ (knew, were).
  • Dấu hiệu bằng chứng trước đó (mắt thâm, vết thương) → present perfect: as if you've been in a fight.
  • Câu quá khứ (He looked …) → mệnh đề sau lùi thì: as if he meant what he said.
  • Không nhầm as if với as (because) hay like + N.

2.4 · Lỗi thường gặp của người Việt ❌/✅

❌ Người Việt hay nói✅ Đúng🔎 Vì sao
You look like sad.You look sad.look + adj, không like
It seems like rain.It looks like it's going to rain.rain ở đây cần mệnh đề
She acts as if she is a queen.… as if she were/was a queen.không thật → quá khứ
He looks as if he is in a fight.… as if he's been in a fight.dấu vết việc đã xảy ra → PP
It sounds as if they argue.… they're having an argument.đang xảy ra → tiếp diễn
I feel as I'm sick.I feel as if I'm going to be sick.thiếu "if"
Don't treat me like I am a kid.… as if I were a kid.không thật → as if + quá khứ
It looks like that she's not coming.It looks like she isn't coming.không thêm "that"

2.5 · Áp dụng vào nói & viết hàng ngày ✍️

  • Phỏng đoán lịch sự: "It looks like we'll have to reschedule the meeting."
  • Hỏi han: "You look as if you need a break. Are you OK?"
  • Kể chuyện: "He looked at me as though I was crazy."
  • IELTS Speaking: "I remember it as if it happened yesterday."
  • Quy tắc vàng: thật/có thể thật → giữ thì thật; không thật → lùi quá khứ.
