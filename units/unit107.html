1.1 · Mục tiêu & bản chất

as … as dùng để nói hai thứ bằng nhau; not as … as nói thứ thứ nhất kém hơn. Unit này còn dạy less … than, twice as … as, the same as và cách dùng đại từ sau than / as (than me hay than I am?).

Sarah: $20m · Joe: $15m · David: $10m
Joe is richer than David. But he isn't as rich as Sarah.
not as rich as Sarah = Sarah giàu hơn Joe.

1.2 · Công thức cốt lõi

(+) as + adj/adv + as It's just as quick as the bus. (-) not as / not so + adj/adv + as He isn't as old as he looks. less + adj + than (≈ not as … as) It was less crowded than usual. twice / three times as … as twice as expensive as the same (+ noun) as the same age as James than me / than I am You're taller than me.
Cấu trúcNghĩaVí dụ
as … asbằngI got here as fast as I could.
not as/so … askhông bằngIt isn't as cold as yesterday.
less … thanít … hơnI spent less money than you.
X times as … asgấp X lầnthree times as big as ours
the same asgiốngHer salary is the same as mine.

⚠️ so … as chỉ dùng trong câu phủ định. Câu khẳng định và câu hỏi luôn là as … as.

1.3 · A. not as … as

  • Jack isn't as old as he looks. (trông già hơn tuổi)
  • The town centre wasn't as crowded as usual.
  • I don't know as many people as you do.
  • 'Fifty pounds?' 'No, not as much as that.'

1.4 · B. less … than

I spent less money than you. = I didn't spend as much money as you. I play tennis less than I used to. = I don't play as much as I used to.

1.5 · C. as … as trong câu khẳng định & twice as … as

  • You can have as much as you want.
  • Let's walk. It's just as quick as taking the bus.
  • Send it as soon as possible.
  • Petrol is twice as expensive as it was.

1.6 · D. the same as

Laura's salary is the same as mine. (not the same like) David is the same age as James. She looks the same as she did ten years ago.

1.7 · E. than me / than I am

Thông dụngCũng đúngÍt dùng
You're taller than me.… than I am.… than I.
He's not as clever as her.… as she is.… as she.
I can't run as fast as him.… as he can.… as he.

2.1 · 8 ví dụ thực tế (đời sống · công việc · thi cử)

📧 Please reply as soon as possible. (email công việc)
🏠 Rent in Hanoi is twice as high as in my hometown. (IELTS Speaking — so sánh)
👀 He isn't as old as he looks. (giao tiếp)
🍚 There's plenty. Take as much as you want. (mời ăn)
🚌 The bus is just as quick as a taxi at this time. (đi lại)
💼 My salary is the same as last year. (công việc)
📉 Fewer people visited the museum in 2020, and it was less popular than before. (IELTS Task 1)
🏃 My brother runs faster than me. (nói về gia đình)

2.2 · Mẹo ghi nhớ 🧠

  • "as đi cặp với as, -er đi cặp với than": as tall as · taller than — không trộn.
  • so … as chỉ sống trong câu phủ định: not so … as.
  • the same AS — không bao giờ "the same like".
  • Gấp đôi = twice as … as (không phải "two times more … as").
  • than me / than I am: dùng tân ngữ một mình, hoặc chủ ngữ + trợ động từ.

2.3 · Mẹo làm bài thi 🎯

  • Viết lại câu: A is taller than B ⇄ B isn't as tall as A — đổi chủ ngữ.
  • more … than ⇄ not as … as với tính từ trái nghĩa: nearer than I thought ⇄ not as far as I thought.
  • less … than ⇄ not as much/as often as.
  • fewer … than ⇄ not as many … as.
  • Trợ động từ sau than/as phải khớp thì: as I did / as I usually am / as we have.

2.5 · Áp dụng vào nói & viết hàng ngày ✍️

  • Nói (2 phút/ngày): so sánh hôm nay với hôm qua: "It's not as hot as yesterday. I'm not as tired as I was."
  • Viết (5 câu/ngày): so sánh 2 điện thoại/2 quán cà phê bằng as … as, less … than, the same as.
  • At work: "I'll get back to you as soon as I can."
  • IELTS Task 1: "Sales in March were twice as high as in January."
  • Quy tắc vàng: thấy chữ "as" đầu tiên → nhớ khép lại bằng chữ "as" thứ hai.
