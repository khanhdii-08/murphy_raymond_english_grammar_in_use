1.1 · Mục tiêu & bản chất

May và might dùng để nói điều gì đó CÓ THỂ đúng (= perhaps, "có lẽ") — mức độ chắc chắn thấp hơn nhiều so với must (Unit 28). Hầu hết trường hợp may = might. Với quá khứ dùng may/might have done. Unit này cũng chỉ ra khác biệt quan trọng giữa might not have (có lẽ đã không) và couldn't have (chắc chắn đã không).

'Where's Ben?' 'He may be in his office.' / 'He might be having lunch.' / 'Ask Ann. She might know.'
Không ai chắc chắn → chỉ đưa ra khả năng (= perhaps he is…).

1.2 · Công thức cốt lõi

HIỆN TẠI may / might (not) + be (true, in his office…) may / might (not) + be + V-ing (working, having…) may / might (not) + V (know, want, work…) QUÁ KHỨ may / might (not) + have + been (asleep…) may / might (not) + have + been + V-ing (feeling…) may / might (not) + have + V3 (known, left, had…) Phủ định: may not · might not (mightn't)
Câu với perhapsCâu với might
Perhaps it is true.It might be true.
Perhaps she doesn't work here any more.She might not work here any more.
Perhaps she was asleep.She may have been asleep.
Perhaps you left it at work.You might have left it at work.
Perhaps he wasn't feeling well.He may not have been feeling well.

1.3 · A. May / might cho hiện tại

  • It may be true. / It might be true. (= perhaps it is true)
  • She might know. / She may know.
  • It may not be true. · She might not work here any more.

1.4 · B. May / might have done cho quá khứ

  • 'Why didn't Kate answer?' 'She may have been asleep.'
  • 'I can't find my phone.' 'You might have left it at work.'
  • 'Why wasn't Amy at the meeting?' 'She might not have known about it.'
  • 'Why was David in a bad mood?' 'He may not have been feeling well.'

1.5 · C. Could tương tự may/might — nhưng couldn't thì KHÁC

Ý nghĩaVí dụ
could / could havecó thể (giống may/might)It's strange, but it could be true.
might not havecó lẽ đã không (50/50)She might not have got my message.
couldn't havechắc chắn đã không (gần 100%)She couldn't have got it — otherwise she'd have replied.

1.6 · Thang độ chắc chắn

100% ── It is true. 95% ── It must be true. (Unit 28) 50% ── It may / might / could be true. 50% ── It may not / might not be true. 5% ── It can't / couldn't be true. (Unit 28) 0% ── It isn't true.

❌ Sai✅ Đúng

2.1 · 8 ví dụ thực tế (đời sống · công việc · thi cử)

🏢 'Where's the manager?' 'She may be in a meeting.' (văn phòng)
🍜 Hung isn't answering. He might be having lunch. (đang làm gì đó)
❓ I don't know the answer. Ask Linh — she might know. (lớp học)
📱 'I can't find my phone.' 'You might have left it in the taxi.' (quá khứ)
😴 She didn't reply last night. She may have been asleep. (quá khứ)
📅 Minh wasn't at the meeting. He might not have known about it. (công việc)
📰 The story sounds strange, but it could be true. (đọc tin tức)
🔥 The police say the fire couldn't have been an accident. (couldn't ≠ might not)

2.2 · Mẹo ghi nhớ 🧠

  • might = perhaps: mọi câu "Perhaps + S + V" đều đổi được sang S + might + V nguyên mẫu.
  • Đổi thì quá khứ: perhaps she went → she might have gone; perhaps she was → she might have been.
  • Phủ định đặt sau might: might not be (không phải might be not).
  • might not have = "có lẽ không" 🤷 · couldn't have = "không đời nào" 🚫.

2.3 · Mẹo làm bài thi 🎯

  • Bài viết lại câu với perhaps: xác định thì của động từ gốc → hiện tại: might + V; đang: might be V-ing; quá khứ: might have V3.
  • Perhaps she was working → might have been working (đừng quên been).
  • Perhaps she had to go → might have had to go.
  • Chọn might not have hay couldn't have: có cụm "otherwise / I'm sure / the police say" → couldn't; có "maybe / possible" → might not.

2.4 · Lỗi thường gặp của người Việt ❌/✅

❌ Người Việt hay nói✅ Đúng🔎 Vì sao

2.5 · Áp dụng vào nói & viết hàng ngày ✍️

  • Nói lịch sự, giảm khẳng định: "I might be wrong, but…" — rất hữu ích khi tranh luận.
  • IELTS Speaking (mô tả ảnh): "They might be waiting for a bus. It may be early morning."
  • Viết: đưa giả thuyết: "One reason may be the high cost of living."
  • Công việc: "The client might not have received the file. Let me check."
