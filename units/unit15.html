1.1 · Mục tiêu & bản chất

Quá khứ hoàn thành (had + V3) dùng khi bạn đang kể một chuyện trong quá khứ và muốn lùi về trước thời điểm đó: "lúc ấy thì việc kia đã xảy ra rồi". Nó là "quá khứ của quá khứ".

When Sarah arrived at the party, Paul had gone home.
Mốc kể chuyện: Sarah đến (11h). Paul về trước đó (10h30) → had gone.

1.2 · Công thức cốt lõi

(+) S + had + V3 She had gone. (= She'd gone) (-) S + had not (hadn't) + V3 He hadn't flown before. (?) Had + S + V3? Had you met him before? Dùng cho MỌI chủ ngữ: I / you / we / they / he / she / it + had
Mốc quá khứ (past simple)Việc xảy ra trước mốc (past perfect)
When we got home last night,we found that somebody had broken into the flat.
Karen didn't want to see the moviebecause she'd already seen it.
The man next to me was nervous.He had never flown before.

⚠️ 'd có thể là had hoặc would: She'd gone (had, vì sau là V3) ↔ She'd go (would, vì sau là V nguyên dạng).

1.3 · A. Điểm xuất phát của câu chuyện

Câu chuyện bắt đầu bằng quá khứ đơn (Sarah arrived...). Muốn nói việc xảy ra trước điểm này → had + V3.

  • At first I thought I'd done the right thing, but I soon realised that I'd made a big mistake.

1.4 · B. So sánh với hiện tại hoàn thành

Present perfect (trước BÂY GIỜ)Past perfect (trước một lúc trong QUÁ KHỨ)
Who is that woman? I've seen her before.I wasn't sure who she was. I'd seen her before.
We aren't hungry. We've just had lunch.We weren't hungry. We'd just had lunch.
The house is dirty. They haven't cleaned it for weeks.The house was dirty. They hadn't cleaned it for weeks.

1.5 · C. So sánh với quá khứ đơn

Past simple (theo sau / cùng lúc)Past perfect (trước đó)
Was Tom there when you arrived? — Yes, but he left soon afterwards. (bạn đến → rồi Tom đi)— No, he had already left. (Tom đi → rồi bạn đến)
Kate wasn't at home when I phoned. She was at her mother's.Kate had just got home when I phoned. She had been at her mother's.

📌 Nếu các việc được kể theo đúng thứ tự xảy ra (A rồi B rồi C) → chỉ cần quá khứ đơn. Chỉ dùng had done khi bạn quay ngược lại việc xảy ra trước.

1.6 · Dấu hiệu nhận biết

Dấu hiệuVí dụ
when / by the time + quá khứ đơn, ... alreadyWhen I arrived, the film had already started.
never / ever ... before (trong câu chuyện quá khứ)I had never seen snow before.
found / realised / knew / thought that ...I realised I had left my bag on the bus.
because + lý do xảy ra trướcShe was upset because she had failed.

1.7 · Lỗi sai điển hình

❌ Sai✅ Đúng
When I arrived, the film already started.When I arrived, the film had already started.
I've never been there before, so I was nervous.I'd never been there before, so I was nervous.
She had went home.She had gone home.
Sorry I'm late. The car had broken down.Sorry I'm late. The car broke down.
I got up, had had breakfast and had left.I got up, had breakfast and left.

2.1 · 8 ví dụ thực tế (đời sống · công việc · thi cử)

🚌 When I got to the bus stop, the bus had already left. (lỡ xe buýt)
🏠 We found that somebody had broken into our flat. (kể chuyện bị trộm)
❄️ I had never seen snow before I went to Japan. (IELTS — kể trải nghiệm)
🍜 I wasn't hungry because I'd just had lunch. (từ chối lời mời ăn)
💼 By the time the meeting started, the boss had read the report. (công việc)
😳 I realised I had made a big mistake. (kể chuyện, nhận ra lỗi)
🏙️ My home town had changed a lot — I hadn't been back for ten years. (kể về quê)
🎬 Mai didn't come to the cinema because she'd already seen the film. (lý do xảy ra trước)

2.2 · Mẹo ghi nhớ 🧠

  • "Đã ... rồi" trong chuyện quá khứ = had + V3. Hỏi: "Lúc đó việc này đã xong chưa?" → rồi → had done.
  • Vẽ trục thời gian: đánh dấu mốc kể chuyện (quá khứ đơn); việc nào nằm bên trái mốc → had done.
  • Had cho mọi ngôi — không có "has had done" hay "hads".
  • Kể theo thứ tự → quá khứ đơn; kể lùi → had done.

2.3 · Mẹo làm bài thi 🎯

  • "When + quá khứ đơn, ..." — đọc kỹ nghĩa: When I arrived, he left (đến rồi anh ấy mới đi) ≠ When I arrived, he had left (anh ấy đi trước khi tôi đến).
  • Thấy by the time + quá khứ đơn → mệnh đề còn lại thường là had done.
  • Thấy already / just / never ... before trong câu kể quá khứ → had done.
  • Đừng lạm dụng: nếu câu đang ở hiện tại (Sorry I'm late) thì việc vừa xảy ra là quá khứ đơn.

2.4 · Lỗi thường gặp của người Việt ❌/✅

❌ Người Việt hay nói✅ Đúng🔎 Vì sao
When I came, she already went out.... she had already gone out.việc đi xảy ra trước khi tôi đến

He had went to bed.He had gone to bed.had + V3

2.5 · Áp dụng vào nói & viết hàng ngày ✍️

  • Kể chuyện (IELTS Part 2): chèn 1–2 câu had done để làm rõ thứ tự: "I'd never travelled alone before, so I was quite nervous."
  • Viết email giải thích: "When I checked the file, someone had already changed the data."
  • Luyện tập: mỗi tối viết 3 câu "When I got home, ... had ...".
  • Nghe phim/truyện: để ý cụm had already, had never, had just — đó là chỗ người kể quay ngược thời gian.
