1.1 · Mục tiêu & bản chất

Unit này là một "bộ sưu tập" các cụm cố định luôn đi với V-ing. Không có quy tắc logic chung — bạn cần học thuộc theo cụm. Tin tốt: chúng xuất hiện cực kỳ nhiều trong giao tiếp và đề thi (đặc biệt it's worth -ing, have difficulty -ing, spend time -ing).

It's only five minutes on foot, so it's not worth taking a taxi.
worth + V-ing = đáng để làm (ở đây: không đáng).

1.2 · Công thức cốt lõi

It's no use / It's no good + V-ing = vô ích There's no point (in) + V-ing = chẳng có lý do gì / chẳng ích gì What's the point of + V-ing? = làm … để làm gì? It's (not) worth + V-ing · N is worth + V-ing = (không) đáng have trouble / difficulty / a problem + V-ing = gặp khó khăn khi spend / waste + time + V-ing = dành / phí thời gian làm be busy + V-ing = bận làm go + V-ing (hoạt động) go swimming, go shopping…
CụmVí dụ
it's no useIt's no use crying about it now.
it's no goodIt's no good trying to call her — her phone is off.
there's no point inThere's no point in buying a gym card if you never go.
the point ofWhat's the point of buying it?
(not) worthThis book is worth reading.
have difficultyI had difficulty finding your house.
spend / waste timeShe spends hours chatting online.
be busyHe's busy preparing for the exam.

1.3 · A. It's no use / It's no good / There's no point

  • The train has gone. It's no use running. · It's no good shouting — nobody can hear you.
  • There's no point (in) -ing: There was no point in waiting, so we left.
  • Nhưng với "the point": the point OF -ing → What's the point of having a car if you never drive it?

1.4 · B. It's (not) worth -ing

  • It's not worth repairing this old phone — buy a new one.
  • Chủ ngữ là vật: The museum is worth visiting. · Was the film worth seeing? · There was nothing worth stealing.

⚠️ Không nói worth to see. Cũng không cần bị động: The book is worth reading (không phải worth being read).

1.5 · C. Have trouble / difficulty / a problem -ing

  • I had no trouble finding a hotel. (không nói trouble to find)
  • Did you have any difficulty getting a visa? · People often have problems reading my handwriting.

1.6 · D. Spend / waste time · be busy

  • spend / waste + thời gian/tiền + -ing: He spent the whole weekend painting the fence. · Don't waste money buying things you don't need.
  • (be) busy -ing: I can't talk now — I'm busy cooking.

1.7 · E. Go swimming / go fishing…

go swimming · go fishing · go shopping · go camping · go jogging · go hiking go skiing · go sailing · go surfing · go riding · go sightseeing · go scuba diving

Chia go theo thì: We went swimming. · She goes jogging every morning. · I've never been skiing. (gone/been: Unit 7D)

1.8 · Lỗi hay gặp

❌ Sai✅ Đúng
It's worth to visit Hoi An.Hoi An is worth visiting. / It's worth visiting Hoi An.
I have difficulty to understand him.I have difficulty understanding him.
I spent two hours to do homework.I spent two hours doing homework.
What's the point in to argue?What's the point of arguing?
Let's go to swim.Let's go swimming.

Liên kết trong sách: Unit 7D (gone / been) · Unit 60 (giới từ + -ing) · Unit 62 (verb + preposition + -ing).

2.1 · 8 ví dụ thực tế (đời sống · công việc · thi cử)

🏮 Hoi An is definitely worth visiting. (du lịch — gợi ý điểm đến)
📱 I waste too much time scrolling through social media. (IELTS Speaking — thói quen)
🗺️ Did you have any trouble finding the office? (công việc — đón khách)
😤 It's no use complaining. The decision has been made. (công việc — họp)
⏳ There's no point in waiting — the shop is closed. (đời sống)
📚 She spends two hours a day practising English. (học tập — mô tả thói quen)
🍳 Sorry, I can't answer the phone — I'm busy cooking. (giao tiếp)
🏕️ We went camping in Ba Vi last weekend. (kể chuyện — go + -ing)

2.2 · Mẹo ghi nhớ 🧠

  • "Vô ích" = no use / no good / no point — cả ba đều + -ing.
  • point IN (There's no point in) nhưng point OF (What's the point of) — nhớ cặp này bằng câu: "There's no point in asking what the point of it is."
  • worth = đáng → worth seeing / reading / trying / visiting — cụm "trải nghiệm" luôn -ing.
  • Thời gian + -ing: spend / waste / be busy → sau đó luôn là hành động -ing.
  • go + -ing = các hoạt động giải trí; đừng nói go to swimming.

2.3 · Mẹo làm bài thi 🎯

  • Viết lại câu: Why work if…? → There's no point in working if…
  • It was difficult to X → I had difficulty X-ing. It wasn't a problem to X → I had no trouble X-ing.
  • It takes me an hour to read the news → I spend an hour reading the news.
  • Thấy worth mà đáp án có "to" → loại ngay.
  • Chia đúng thì của go: goes / went / gone / been + -ing.

2.5 · Áp dụng vào nói & viết hàng ngày ✍️

  • Gợi ý du lịch: giới thiệu 3 nơi ở quê bạn: … is worth visiting because…
  • IELTS Speaking Part 1: "How do you spend your free time?" → I spend most evenings reading / I often go jogging.
  • Công việc: I had some difficulty accessing the file. Could you resend it?
  • Tự nhắc mình: viết lên giấy note: It's no use worrying about things I can't change.
