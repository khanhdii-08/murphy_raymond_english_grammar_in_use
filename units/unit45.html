1.1 · Mục tiêu & bản chất

Khi muốn truyền đạt lời đồn, nhận định chung, tin tức chưa kiểm chứng mà không nói rõ ai nói, tiếng Anh dùng bị động của các động từ say, believe, think, expect, report…. Unit này cũng dạy be supposed to — một cụm cực kỳ phổ biến trong văn nói với hai nghĩa khác nhau.

Nobody knows exactly how old the tree is, but:
It is said that it is 500 years old. = It is said to be 500 years old.
Cả hai câu = People say that it is 500 years old. Người nói không khẳng định chắc chắn.

1.2 · Công thức cốt lõi

Cách 1: It + is/was + V3 (said, believed…) + that + mệnh đề It is said that he speaks five languages. Cách 2: S + is/was + V3 + to + V (cùng thời điểm / tương lai) He is said to speak five languages. S + is/was + V3 + to be + V-ing (đang diễn ra) The boy is believed to be wearing a red T-shirt. S + is/was + V3 + to have + V3 (xảy ra TRƯỚC đó) He is alleged to have stolen the money.
It is … that …S + is … to …
It is said that she runs 10 km a day.She is said to run 10 km a day.
It is believed that the boy is wearing a cap.The boy is believed to be wearing a cap.
It is expected that the strike will end soon.The strike is expected to end soon.
It is alleged that he hit a policeman.He is alleged to have hit a policeman.
It is said that there is a tunnel here.There is said to be a tunnel here.
It is reported that two people were injured.Two people are reported to have been injured.

📰 Động từ hay dùng: saidbelievedthoughtconsideredexpectedknownreportedallegedunderstood — rất phổ biến trong tin tức.

1.3 · A. Chọn dạng nguyên mẫu đúng (cách 2)

  • Mệnh đề that ở hiện tại đơn / will → to + V: is expected to end.
  • Mệnh đề that ở tiếp diễn → to be + V-ing: is said to be losing money.
  • Mệnh đề that ở quá khứ / hoàn thành → to have + V3: is thought to have escaped.
  • Mệnh đề that ở bị động quá khứ → to have been + V3: is reported to have been damaged.

1.4 · B. Supposed to = said to (nghe nói)

I want to see that film. It's supposed to be good. (= people say it's good) Fireworks are supposed to have been invented in China.

1.5 · C. Supposed to = lẽ ra phải / theo kế hoạch là

Nói về điều được dự định, sắp xếp, mong đợi — thường khác với thực tế:

  • The plan is supposed to be a secret, but everyone knows.
  • What are you doing at work? You're supposed to be on holiday.
  • The bus was supposed to leave at 8, but it was late.
  • I'd better go. I'm supposed to be meeting Linh in ten minutes.

1.6 · D. Not supposed to = không được phép / không nên

You're not supposed to park here. It's private. (= không được phép) He's still not supposed to lift heavy things. (= không nên, theo lời bác sĩ)

1.7 · Lỗi hay gặp

❌ Sai✅ Đúng
He is said that he is rich.He is said to be rich. / It is said that he is rich.
The thief is believed to escape yesterday.… is believed to have escaped yesterday.
You are not suppose to smoke here.You are not supposed to smoke here.
The train supposed to arrive at 6.The train was supposed to arrive at 6.

Liên kết trong sách: Units 42–44 (Passive) · Unit 47 (reported speech) · Unit 54 (to … / to have done).

2.1 · 8 ví dụ thực tế (đời sống · công việc · thi cử)

📰 Three people are reported to have been injured in the fire. (tin tức — bị động tường thuật)
🌪️ The storm is expected to reach the coast tonight. (dự báo thời tiết)
🏛️ Ha Long Bay is considered to be one of the wonders of nature. (IELTS Writing — trang trọng)
🍜 This pho place is supposed to be the best in town. (đời sống — nghe đồn)
⏰ The meeting was supposed to start at 9, but the boss was late. (công việc — kế hoạch khác thực tế)
🚭 You're not supposed to smoke in here. (quy định — không được phép)
🕵️ The suspect is believed to be hiding in the mountains. (tin tức — đang diễn ra)
📈 It is thought that house prices will rise again next year. (IELTS — nêu quan điểm chung)

2.2 · Mẹo ghi nhớ 🧠

  • Hai cửa vào: bắt đầu bằng It → dùng that; bắt đầu bằng người/vật → dùng to. Không trộn hai cách.
  • "Lùi xa hơn → have V3": việc xảy ra trước lúc người ta nói → to have + V3.
  • Supposed to có 2 mặt: nghe nói (said to) và lẽ ra / theo kế hoạch. Nhìn ngữ cảnh: có but… phía sau thường là nghĩa "lẽ ra".
  • Phát âm: supposed to /səˈpəʊs tə/ — chữ d gần như không nghe thấy nhưng vẫn phải viết.

2.3 · Mẹo làm bài thi 🎯

  • Bài viết lại câu: xác định thì trong mệnh đề that → chọn to V / to be V-ing / to have V3 / to have been V3.
  • Nếu câu gốc có there is → There is said to be…
  • Cấu trúc này giúp bài IELTS Writing trang trọng hơn: It is widely believed that…
  • Thấy be supposed to + quá khứ (was/were) → hầu như luôn là điều đã không xảy ra như kế hoạch.

2.4 · Lỗi thường gặp của người Việt ❌/✅

❌ Người Việt hay nói✅ Đúng🔎 Vì sao
She is said that she is very kind.She is said to be very kind.chủ ngữ người → to V
It is said him to be rich.It is said that he is rich.It … that + mệnh đề
He is thought to leave the country last week.He is thought to have left …quá khứ → to have V3
The road is expected to finish next year.The road is expected to be finished …con đường được hoàn thành → bị động
People are said that…It is said that… / People say that…trộn chủ động và bị động
You are not suppose to…You are not supposed to…luôn có -ed
He supposed to call me.He was supposed to call me.thiếu be
I supposed to be at work now.I'm supposed to be at work now.thiếu am
There is said that a ghost lives here.It is said that … / There is said to be a ghost here.there + said to be

2.5 · Áp dụng vào nói & viết hàng ngày ✍️

  • Gợi ý quán ăn / phim: That new café is supposed to be really good.
  • Phàn nàn nhẹ nhàng: The delivery was supposed to arrive yesterday.
  • Nhắc quy định: We're not supposed to use phones during the exam.
  • Đọc báo: mỗi ngày tìm 2 câu is said / is believed / is reported to… và viết lại theo cách còn lại.
